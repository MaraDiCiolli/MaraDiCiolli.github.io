Hello, I'm Mara.

Geotechnical engineer, PE · MASc candidate at UBC · I think a lot about rocks so you don't have to.

I'm a geotechnical engineer who's spent 7+ years figuring out how to keep underground mines from doing things they shouldn't — like collapsing. I've worked on everything from open pit slope stability to machine-learning-powered hazard maps for rock bursts, mostly with Freeport-McMoRan across their underground operations in Arizona and New Mexico.

Right now I'm at the University of British Columbia working on my Master's, where I'm researching how rock fragmentation affects production in cave mines. Think of it as solving a very large, very heavy puzzle — underground, in the dark. Before that, I earned my BS in Mining Engineering from the University of Arizona (summa cum laude, because rocks deserved my full attention).

I'm passionate about making mines safer, smarter, and more predictable. When I'm not deep in geotechnical data, I'm probably exploring the mountains above ground for a change.
